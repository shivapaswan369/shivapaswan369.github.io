Customer Data Cleanup

A small before-and-after demonstration of organizing inconsistent customer records in a spreadsheet.

Practice sample only: All records are fictional. This is self-created demonstration work, not a paid client project. Email addresses use example.com test domains.

What was inconsistent?

  • Names and email addresses had mixed capitalization and extra spaces.
  • Dates used several formats.
  • Payment statuses had inconsistent capitalization and trailing spaces.
  • Some phone values were missing.
  • Two repeated records were identified by normalized email address.

Cleanup steps

  • Trimmed unnecessary spaces and standardized customer-name capitalization.
  • Converted email addresses to lowercase.
  • Standardized dates to YYYY-MM-DD.
  • Standardized payment-status capitalization.
  • Removed two duplicate records using normalized email as the matching key.
  • Kept the missing phone value visible as “Not provided” rather than guessing it.

Scope and limitations

  • This example demonstrates basic data cleanup and formatting only.
  • Email values were normalized, not verified as deliverable.
  • Duplicate matching used normalized email addresses; ambiguous conflicts were not researched against real sources.
  • No live business data, customer system, macros, VBA, or automated refresh is involved.
