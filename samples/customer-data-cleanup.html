Customer Data Cleanup

A basic before-and-after spreadsheet cleanup example using fictional customer records.

What was done

  • Trimmed extra spaces from names, emails and city fields.
  • Standardized names and city capitalization.
  • Converted email addresses to lowercase.
  • Removed spaces and hyphens from phone numbers.
  • Converted mixed date formats into YYYY-MM-DD.
  • Removed one exact duplicate record.
