01. Overview

Welcome to Shiva's Portfolio, a personal website showcasing Excel projects, data organization work, and learning progress. Your privacy is important to us. This policy describes the information practices associated with this website.

02. Information We Collect

You can browse most of this website without directly providing personal information. However, certain information may be processed when you interact with the website.

  • Usage data: Pages visited, approximate location, device type, browser, and referral source, where analytics services provide this information.
  • Contact information: If you email us, we may receive your email address and the information you choose to include in your message.
  • Technical data: Basic technical information may be processed by the website hosting provider to deliver and protect the site.

Please do not send passwords, payment details, or other sensitive personal information through general contact messages.

03. Analytics & Cookies

This website may use Google Analytics to understand website traffic, visitor interactions, and which pages are useful. Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies, depending on the service configuration.

Google Analytics data is processed under Google's applicable terms and privacy policies. You can learn more at Google's Privacy Policy.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some cookies may affect certain website features.

04. How Information Is Used

Information, where collected, may be used to:

  • Operate and maintain the website.
  • Understand visitor activity and improve website content.
  • Respond to messages and enquiries.
  • Detect technical issues, misuse, or security threats.

We do not claim to sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers as needed to operate the site.

05. Third-Party Services

This website is hosted through GitHub Pages and may use Google Analytics. These providers may process technical or usage information according to their own policies.

External links may lead to websites that we do not control. Please review their privacy policies before sharing information with them.

  • GitHub Privacy Policies
  • Google Privacy Policy

06. Data Security & Retention

Reasonable steps are taken to keep the website operating safely. However, no website or method of electronic transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Information handled by third-party services is retained according to their respective settings, policies, and retention practices. Emails may remain in inboxes or backups until deleted.

07. Your Choices

You may choose not to send personal information and can manage cookies through your browser. Depending on the applicable law, you may have additional rights concerning your personal data.

This policy should accurately reflect the services actually enabled on the website. If advertising, embedded content, or additional analytics tools are added later, this policy may need to be updated.
